What the camera measures

Angles and visible fingertip positions are compared with your own reference. Torso and shoulder drift cost more points than the same change in elbow angle. Each posture grade combines average accuracy (75%) with accuracy during the least consistent quarter of visible samples (25%). For strings, keep the instrument aligned with the grid. The view estimates the nearest string; it cannot verify contact or pressure. Video stays in this browser.
